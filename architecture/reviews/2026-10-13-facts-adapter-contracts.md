# dev96 Facts adapter contract acceptance

Status: accepted implementation subset. Final frozen regression, independent
installation, original-version compatibility and same-reviewer closure passed.
Final document/package checks and exact local delivery are recorded separately;
full R25/R01–R26 and actual quality/adoption gates remain open.

## Scope and authority

Facts-store/v1 core selects F01–F10; managed selects all F01–F16. Current reads,
CAS and TTL are distinct from bounded recall/validation/exact inspection, and
scanner/index/vector/embedding remain separate roles. Knowledge and Session v1
catalogs/reports/default identities stay fixed. Quality fields remain null.

An independent editable sqlite3 reference and configured starter exercise the
original Local Agent, MemoryPipeline and ManagedMemoryPolicy. Original business
completion, auxiliary unknown writes, exact inspection and reservation settlement
remain separate facts; no alternative execution authority is introduced.

Core certifies typed valid caller inputs and completed values. Raw native input
coercion and different canonical-value checks in existing raw validators are not
silently standardized. The scoped FactsView and original adopted-reference
boundary carry their existing provenance/currentness responsibilities. Full read
and explicitly bounded recall paths are not assumed to have identical contracts.

## Fresh review and repair status

One fresh whole-batch reviewer found three P2 false failures in the new fixed
cases, with independent reproductions: short expiry before slow recall could
misclassify a correctly hidden fact; suffixes could exceed a valid 128-character
namespace; historical close counts could misclassify an open borrowed source.
The original case author repaired these boundaries, including shared audit-dict
aliasing and whitespace-only truncated prefixes. All three findings are closed
by the same reviewer after five unchanged independent probes and 15 final
closure tests. The author recorded meaningful failures before repairs and 206
focused case/pipeline/managed/binding checks after repairs. Native methods and
execution authority remain unchanged. Final frozen regression passes 7989 checks /
617 configured skips in 1362.69s; original PID 3551 was waited for and reaped
with exit 0. All 342 product and 591 test modules stayed equal to the start
snapshot.

Independent core and SDK installations each pass all 262 new contract/default/
configuration/recovery checks, with warnings treated as errors, caching disabled
and no source fallback. Each also passes the separate 50-test reference/recovery
lane (overlaps the 262); actual process exits, source closure and original
reservations are retained. Both generated applications complete 24 unique-command
mode/configuration/ownership combinations in one dataset. Original dev95
compatibility passes 16 same-Run/Turn roundtrips and 80 reaped child processes,
with native methods/defaults and Knowledge/Session metadata/report bytes equal.
These independent records identify the candidate/repaired wheel bytes; final
documentation packaging will preserve the tested product modules.

## Evidence gates

- Fixed catalogs and literal Knowledge/Session bytes, truthful missing-hook reports.
- Eight independent Scope/namespace addresses, actual CAS/TTL/detachment/errors.
- Completed Local read adoption and original bounded post-await expiry checks.
- Actual suspended calls and cancellation during prepare/seed/fault/pipeline,
  failed cleanup retention, original thread/group drain before closing resources.
- Original partial pipeline command/fact replay and managed lost-ACK/receipt
  settlement recovery, including actual independent reaped process windows.
- SDK/JSON/TOML, sync/async, owned/borrowed and mixed-mode generated application.
- One fresh whole-batch reviewer and same-reviewer repair closure; final frozen
  source regression, independent wheel installs and dev95 original compatibility.
- Source/wheel/sdist/installed/documentation/CI provenance and exact local delivery.

These gates do not certify arbitrary remote clients, scan/index lifecycle, model
or semantic quality, independent adoption, full R25/H3/M1/R01–R26, or enterprise
formal deployment. Those requirements remain in the standing capability queue.
