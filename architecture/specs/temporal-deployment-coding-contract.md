Provide pure `coding_call_candidate(state, identity)` for an unended call that exactly matches an original selected Coding Tool Activity carrier digest and action. It requires the recorded original Tool invocation, original native owner and durable `coding_owner_stop_proof`, full native fence/pending-stop closure, and stopped original Tool owner. Original History derives the candidate; its Update schedules verification on the first actual queue/timeout. ## Owned lifecycle

Close first stops new owned SDK submissions, then durably closes the matching Coding acceptance index and fences/reconciles its original native/tree resources. Preserve Worker, receivers, SDK clients and PG until original physical and ACK evidence is adopted. Then drain deployment actual calls and resources; stop Worker and close owned clients last. Failed close retains original resources and evidence; borrowed clients remain caller-owned.

Closing business admission must preserve the exact registered maintenance path. The owned Worker may finish its runtime/client/source-pinned release/verify and original Coding-stop handlers; selected3 may also finish original CONTROL observe/cancel through the existing SDK ancestry, actual-call and end-ACK wrappers. Register, recover and other new business handlers remain refused. Keep both actual-call trackers available until original Histories have adopted queued completions and eligible roots have a positively verified original Registry retirement. A temporary not-ready projection is not unknown-effect evidence. Physical-only close with a held root requires explicit original unknown debt and no remaining transient adoption in its subtree; it does not certify retirement or discard recovery data.

A child's physical-stop proof does not settle its business result or credits. A selected3 Parent with an unsettled, physically stopped child must still consume a fresh validated child notification through the existing dirty-generation observation gate. An unchanged child remains idle; a notification neither grants new business nor substitutes for original result, physical, budget or closure proofs.

An attached owned Assembly retains its original SDK queue. If the same Registry has never started, owned close may initialize that Registry3 at its fixed deployment address, using the original policy and namespace, `REJECT_DUPLICATE`, `FAIL` and no Workflow retry. It must positively verify the original RUNNING SDK epoch and configuration before the Registry-owned scoped-close Update. A racing start or uncertain reply does not authorize a replacement. Existing Registry2, reset or terminated Histories refuse. Empty candidates are read from this durable authority, never inferred from missing History or empty PG rows. Close without a fixed owned queue and all diagnostic queries remain unable to create a Registry.

A never-attached Assembly can release its owned components only through the existing strict unused-resource gate: no SDK client, Worker, Engine, owned submission, actual call, active native handler or recorded resource. This is component cleanup and makes no accepted-Run stop claim. A positively sealed child is not sent a new cancel; its original SDK epoch and physical proof are still verified and adopted by the unsealed parent.

Diagnostics for publication3 are read-only, including missing Registry: they do not start a Workflow or invoke native query/stop. Return explicit missing state or typed NotFound, never a fabricated durable receipt. Submission/close/recovery retain exact original Scope and first release even after alias movement.
